# Account / Family Ledger Account Integration Design

Status date: 2026-06-12

Task type: design-only. This document does not implement SQL, RPC, API, or UI changes.

## Goal

Define how v2 should extend the current account system so school, store, and future family-ledger accounts can coexist without mixing company money, personal money, family money, advances, reimbursements, transfers, and reporting.

## Current Baseline

- Existing account master data lives in `school_accounts`.
- Existing cash movement audit lives in `school_account_transactions`.
- Current school account profile create/edit is narrowed:
  - create fields: `name`, `currency`, currency-linked `account_type`, `business_entity_id`, initial balance, `is_company_account`, `is_active`, `note`;
  - edit fields: `name`, `currency`, currency-linked `account_type`, `business_entity_id`, `is_company_account`, `is_active`, `note`;
  - `account_code` is generated by RPC;
  - current balance is display-only;
  - initial balance is create-only;
  - currency change is rejected once account transactions exist.
- Current account types are pre-aligned for future household use:
  - `CNY`: 余额宝, 余利宝;
  - `JPY`: 日元三菱卡, 日元乐天卡, 日元现金.

## Recommendation

Use one shared account ledger model with explicit scope isolation, rather than duplicating unrelated account tables per app.

Recommended long-term structure:

- `app_type`: domain boundary, for example `school`, `store`, `family`.
- `account_scope`: ownership/use boundary, for example `company`, `personal`, `family`, `advance`.
- `business_entity_id`: required for business-scoped `school` / `store` accounts; not the owner key for family accounts.
- future `household_id` / `family_member_id` or equivalent: required for `family` accounts when the family module is introduced.

The current `school_accounts` / `school_account_transactions` can either be evolved into shared `accounts` / `account_transactions`, or kept as physical table names with explicit `app_type` and scope guards. The key design requirement is not the table name; it is that every write and read path filters by `app_type` and ownership scope.

## Scope Isolation

### Company Accounts

- Represent business-owned funds.
- Require business ownership, currently `business_entity_id`.
- Can be used for school/store income, expense, wage payment, reimbursements, and transfers within the same app/business scope.
- Must never be used for family expenses or family income.

### Personal / Advance Accounts

- Represent individual funds used temporarily for business operations.
- Should be explicit `account_scope = advance` or equivalent, not inferred only from `is_company_account = false`.
- Business expense paid by an advance account may become reimbursement candidate.
- Reimbursement repays the advance account from a company account and must preserve source expense, reimbursement item, and account transaction audit records.

### Family Accounts

- Represent household funds.
- Use `app_type = family` plus a future household/member owner key.
- Family income/expense/transfer records stay inside family ledger reporting.
- Family accounts must not appear as school/store payment, reimbursement, wage, income, expense, or profit-summary account candidates.

### Personal Non-Business Accounts

- If the family ledger wants personal accounts not owned by the business, model them under `app_type = family` or a future personal finance app scope.
- Do not put them in school/store business account selectors.

## Preventing Mixed Accounting

Required guards for future implementation:

- Ordinary account transfer:
  - allow only same `app_type`;
  - allow only compatible owner context;
  - require same currency unless a dedicated FX transfer workflow is designed;
  - reject family-to-school and school-to-family transfers in the ordinary transfer RPC.
- Business expense:
  - account must belong to the same business app and business entity;
  - family accounts are not candidates.
- Family expense:
  - account must be `app_type = family`;
  - record must not create school/store expense rows.
- Company reimbursement:
  - from account must be company/business-owned;
  - to account must be approved advance/personal-business account;
  - family accounts are excluded.
- Summary/profit reports:
  - school/store profit reports read only business income/expense;
  - family ledger reports read only family income/expense;
  - account transactions remain audit evidence, not the primary classifier for operating profit.

## Account Type Expansion

Do not keep account type options hardcoded long-term.

Recommended future table/config:

- `account_type_code`
- `label`
- `currency`
- `app_type`
- `account_scope`
- `sort_order`
- `is_active`

Initial seed examples:

- `cny_yuebao`, CNY, family/personal-compatible, 余额宝
- `cny_yulibao`, CNY, family/personal-compatible, 余利宝
- `jpy_mufg_card`, JPY, family/personal-compatible, 日元三菱卡
- `jpy_rakuten_card`, JPY, family/personal-compatible, 日元乐天卡
- `jpy_cash`, JPY, family/personal-compatible, 日元现金

School/store business accounts may later add business-specific bank/cash/wallet types without exposing those to family users.

## Balance Rules

- `current_balance` stays display-only.
- Initial balance appears only on account creation.
- Creation may initialize `opening_balance` and `current_balance` before any account transaction exists.
- After creation, balance changes should be audit records, not direct profile edits.

Recommended future balance tools:

1. Balance adjustment:
   - creates account adjustment record;
   - creates account transaction;
   - updates `current_balance`;
   - requires reason and operator metadata;
   - excluded from operating profit unless explicitly categorized otherwise.
2. Opening-balance correction:
   - allowed only before any real business/family transactions, or as a dedicated correction workflow that creates an explicit `opening_balance_correction` audit transaction;
   - must not rewrite existing transaction history;
   - must be separate from ordinary account profile edit.

## Impact Scope

### Tables

Potential future changes:

- add `account_scope` to account master;
- add owner-context columns for family/household if family ledger is implemented;
- optionally introduce account-type config;
- ensure account transactions carry enough source metadata to audit app/scope/owner.

Do not migrate or backfill existing real account history without a separate guarded migration design.

### RPC

Potential future RPCs/guards:

- account create/update should validate `app_type`, `account_scope`, currency/type compatibility, and owner context;
- transfer/adjustment RPCs should reject cross-app and incompatible-scope writes;
- reimbursement RPCs should explicitly allow only business advance-account repayment;
- family income/expense RPCs should write only family-scoped records and transactions.

### API

Potential future API changes:

- account fetch helpers must accept explicit app/scope filters;
- selector helpers must return only accounts valid for the current workflow;
- profit/family summaries should not reuse account transaction type alone as the business classifier.

### Pages

Potential future pages:

- school account page continues business account management;
- family ledger account page filters to `app_type = family`;
- shared account components can be reused only if they receive explicit scope config.

## Risks

- Overloading `business_entity_id` for family ownership would mix concepts and make reporting brittle.
- Keeping only `is_company_account` is not enough to distinguish personal advance accounts from family/personal ledger accounts.
- Cross-scope transfers can accidentally become hidden capital contributions or reimbursements if not modeled explicitly.
- Current balance repairs are high risk if implemented as direct edits.
- Profit reports can become wrong if family records reuse school income/expense tables without scope guards.

## Phased Implementation

1. Read-only schema and data audit:
   - inventory account table columns, transaction references, report filters, and account selectors;
   - confirm current `app_type` usage and historical account-type values.
2. Account scope schema design:
   - design `account_scope`, owner-context, and account-type config;
   - define compatibility rules and indexes.
3. RPC guard phase:
   - update account create/edit, transfer, adjustment, reimbursement, income, and expense candidate queries to require app/scope.
4. UI/API phase:
   - update account selectors and filters;
   - add family ledger account page only after scope guards are in place.
5. Balance correction phase:
   - implement balance adjustment/opening correction as audited workflows, not profile edits.
6. Reporting phase:
   - add family summaries separately from business profit summaries;
   - verify reports exclude wrong app/scope records.

## Open Questions

- Whether `store` should share the exact same business entity table or get its own owner model.
- Whether family ledger needs household/member ownership in v1 or can start with a single family scope.
- Whether FX transfers should be supported, and if so whether they require a dedicated exchange-rate transaction pair.
- Whether existing `is_company_account` should be retained as compatibility display or replaced by `account_scope` in UI copy.
