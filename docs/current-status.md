# Current Status

Status date: 2026-06-14

This is the lightweight daily entry document. It intentionally keeps only the current system state, hard stops, safety rules, active backlog, and the latest 5 key updates. Older status history is archived in `docs/archive/current-status-history.md`.

## Current System State

- v2 is currently focused on current/future operations. Historical maintenance remains in v1 or in separately authorized guarded migration/repair phases.
- v2 master-data policy: open create/edit where safe, keep delete/merge closed unless a future audit-safe workflow explicitly opens it.
- Core business writes are DB/RPC-backed. Page modules must not call Supabase `.rpc()` directly and must not directly insert/update/delete/upsert rows; page writes go through `js/api/*-api.js` wrappers and verified RPCs.
- Student settlement, teacher wage generation/snapshots, payment requests, reimbursements, account transactions, income/expense, and locking flows are protected main chains. Master-data dialog work must not mutate these chains.
- Cash linkage v1 business policy is unified as of 2026-06-14: School is the business ledger and keeps business ownership; Cash System remains the user's household/private account ledger and records actual user-controlled account movement. Any money that actually passes through a user-controlled account should enter Cash System, regardless of whether the School business ownership is personal business or 青空塾. Business ownership no longer decides whether a row enters Cash. School initiates external Cash requests from business flows; Cash only accepts external requests, shows them for user approve/reject, and changes Cash transactions/balances only after approve. Cash must not proactively create School business records or initiate School business requests. Current code may still contain older personal-only / JPY-only guards; those are implementation gaps to be corrected before real 2026-05 wage trial resumes.
- Current or unclosed real business months must not be used for real wage generation, snapshot generation, student settlement closing, locking, or lock-style write validation. Validation should use transaction rollback or clearly marked whitelist data such as `codex-test`, `v2-test`, `sandbox`, `测试学生`, `测试老师`, or `测试业务归属`.
- Field narrowing policy: create/edit dialogs should expose only fields actually used in current operations. Historical, reserved, low-frequency, generated, derived, financial-chain, and audit fields stay hidden/readonly but are not physically removed from DB unless a separate cleanup phase is designed. Repeated master-data dialog narrowing tasks should follow `docs/workflows/v2-master-dialog-simplification.md`.

## Hard Stops

Stop and report immediately for:

- missing `SUPABASE_DB_URL`, unavailable `psql`, static check failure, rollback/commit test failure, abnormal git status, or unclear ownership of test data that cannot be solved by creating safe test data;
- need for non-whitelisted real business data, current/unclosed real-month write validation, broad historical-data modification, historical repair, broad backfill, destructive cleanup, `delete`, `truncate`, `drop`, broad permission changes, or irreversible production operation;
- secrets exposure risk, page-level direct DB writes, page-level direct `.rpc()`, non-target module changes, broad refactor, or documentation/request conflict that cannot be safely interpreted.

## Latest Key Updates

1. Final Cash/School business boundary and request policy, 2026-06-14:
   Fixed the final boundary: School System is the business ledger for tuition income, teacher wages, student/teacher/month, business ownership, cost attribution, corporate-account clearing records, and company expense records. Cash System remains the user's household/private account ledger, but because real school money often passes through user-controlled accounts, it records actual Alipay, JPY cash, Mitsubishi/Rakuten, RMB account, CNY/JPY allocation, corporate transfer, and corporate reimbursement movements. School records business facts; Cash records real account changes. School sends external requests; Cash displays pending requests and only approve creates a Cash transaction and changes balance. Reject creates no transaction and changes no balance. Cash does not proactively generate School requests or create School business records. Profit statistics count real operating tuition income, teacher wages, and real business expenses; they must not count Cash-to-corporate transfers, corporate reimbursements to Cash, CNY/JPY exchange, user-account transfers, entrusted tuition clearing, or wage-advance recovery as profit income/expense.

1. Cash linkage v1 unified business policy correction, 2026-06-14:
   Fixed the business policy: School records business ownership and settlement semantics; Cash System records actual account movements for all money that passes through user-controlled accounts. Tuition income enters School for business attribution whether it is personal business, 青空塾, JPY, or CNY/RMB; if the money is received in Alipay, JPY cash, or a JPY bank account, Cash records the corresponding account income. 青空塾 tuition is not excluded from Cash; Cash should label it as `青空塾代收学费`, and later corporate transfer should be recorded as `转给法人账户 / 学费提交 / 代收款清算`, not ordinary household expense. Teacher wage payments also all go through Cash when paid from user-controlled accounts, including personal, 青空塾, mixed ownership, JPY cash/bank, and RMB/Alipay payments; School records personal/青空塾/adjustment/transport/classroom cost attribution, while Cash records the actual payment account. 青空塾 wage payments are treated as `青空塾工资垫付`; later corporate reimbursement is recorded as `法人账户报销 / 青空塾工资垫付报销`. CNY/JPY exchange and account rebalancing are manual Cash operations for now, usually monthly, and are not automatically handled by School. Deprecated old policies include: only personal business enters Cash, 青空塾 does not enter Cash, only personal + `teacher_wage` + JPY links Cash, and only personal tuition JPY links Cash.

1. Cash linkage v2 UI/business direction correction, 2026-06-13:
   Corrected the v2 product direction: School will not add a standalone sync entry. The only real School entry points are the income record page and teacher wage payment page. Personal-business `tuition` JPY income should choose a Cash 收款账户 in the income form, create the school income record, and submit a Cash pending confirmation request as part of that business submission. Personal-business `teacher_wage` JPY payment chooses a Cash 支付账户 in the payment confirmation flow and uses `提交到 Cash 确认`; School must not mark the payment request paid until Cash approves. School UI should use business statuses `Cash待确认`, `Cash已确认`, and `Cash已拒绝`, and must not expose sync executor / pending event terminology to ordinary users. Cash System keeps the `外部待确认` page as the approve/reject entry. The teacher wage payment page now calls `request-cash-confirmation` through `js/api/payment-api.js` for personal + `teacher_wage` + JPY pending requests. The function path creates/reuses the School linkage event, creates a Cash pending request, and moves School to `awaiting_cash_confirmation`; it still does not create a Cash transaction or mark the payment request `paid`. A minimal Supabase Auth login block now exists on the teacher wage payment page so School users can sign in with email/password before invoking JWT-protected Edge Functions. The Cash confirmation action is guarded: unauthenticated users see `请先登录后再提交到 Cash 确认。` and the Function is not called. This is only an operator login/session bridge, not a student/teacher account system or role model.

2. Cash linkage v2 Edge Function bridge code, 2026-06-13:
   Added `supabase/functions/request-cash-confirmation/index.ts` as the School-click backend bridge for page-driven Cash confirmation. The function currently supports only personal-business `teacher_wage` JPY payment requests, not the income page. Cash approve/reject callback writeback code exists but is not deployed or DB-tested yet.

4. Cash linkage v2 direction: page-driven Cash confirmation, 2026-06-13:
   Reframed the long-term Cash linkage design. The current verified chain is `待支付工资 -> School 确认支付 -> payment request paid -> School linkage event pending -> zsh directly creates Cash transaction -> School synced`. This is useful for validation and operations, but it is not the final business workflow. The corrected v2 chain embeds Cash confirmation into the real School business actions: `老师工资支付页面选择 Cash 支付账户 -> 提交到 Cash 确认 -> Cash 外部待确认 approve/reject -> approve creates Cash transaction and changes balance -> School paid/Cash已确认; reject creates no transaction and leaves the payment pending/Cash已拒绝`. For tuition income, `收入记录页面选择 Cash 收款账户 -> 创建收入并提交到 Cash 确认 -> Cash approve/reject` follows the same confirmation model. Cash balance must only change after Cash-side approval. Idempotency moves forward to request creation, while Cash transactions still use the existing external/idempotency guard. The recommended bridge is a Supabase Edge Function; do not make the browser directly write the Cash project, and do not make the Cash frontend directly read the School DB.
